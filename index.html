Products to Source — the Gap Map

Where nobody's selling yet in Malaysia — a saturation-scored shortlist of pet products rising globally but still thin on MY shelves, so we import into open water, not a price war. Cat-weighted, because Malaysia is cat-first. Firecrawl sweep + local TikTok validation · 24 Jul 2026 · directional, validate on Shopee/Lazada MY before committing.

🟢 Tier A — the real openings thin locally · light & cheap to ship · cat-first · ✓ TikTok-validated · start here

Tier A

Cat-specific snuffle / foraging mat

0 cat-framed MY listings. Snuffle mats sell in MY — but almost entirely as dog products. Re-framed for cats (foraging enrichment, slows fast eaters, fights boredom) it's an untouched niche in a cat-first market. Strong video/demo appeal.

✓ TikTok: 150K–256K views but foreign sellers & DIY "make your own" only — no MY seller found.

1688 ¥8–25 · retail RM29–49 · margin ~60% · foldable, light

Tier A

Tilted whisker-friendly anti-vomit bowl

0 MY listings. A tilt + wide shallow dish that stops "whisker fatigue" and the scarf-and-barf vomiting cats are famous for. Solves a real, relatable pain — perfect story-driven product.

✓ TikTok: MY creators voice the pain ("makan terlalu laju boleh menyebabkan muntah"); buyers can't find the elevated model. Demand + scarcity both confirmed.

1688 ¥6–18 · retail RM25–39 · margin ~62% · light, stackable

Tier A

Silvervine / matatabi sticks & chews

Thin vs. catnip; mostly foreign. The "stronger-than-catnip" natural enrichment trend. Semi-consumable (chew sticks wear down → repeat buys), feather-light, cheap to source, easy to bundle. MY catnip is crowded; silvervine is not.

✓ TikTok: up to 1.4M views; works on ~80% of cats even catnip-ignorers, plus dental & hairball benefits. Only one tiny local video.

1688 ¥3–10 · retail RM15–29 · margin ~68% · tiny, repeat
